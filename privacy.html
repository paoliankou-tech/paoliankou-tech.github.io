Privacy Policy

Aline is a private content-publishing tool operated by Phénix Cash Malin. It creates short educational videos and publishes them to the operator's own accounts on TikTok and YouTube. Aline is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.

Data Aline accesses

  • Basic profile information of the operator's own TikTok account (display name, avatar), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Google after the operator authorizes Aline, used only to upload and publish the operator's own videos.
  • Public statistics of the videos Aline published (views, likes, comments), used to improve future videos.

How data is stored

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent to third parties, except to the TikTok and Google APIs themselves when publishing.

Data of viewers

Aline does not collect, store, or process any personal data of people who watch or comment on the videos.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions) or in the Google account permissions page. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the operator's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Phénix Cash Malin channels: YouTube @PhenixCashMalin.
